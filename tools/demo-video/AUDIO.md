# ElevenLabs audio revision

All audio in the revised film must come from ElevenLabs: narration, music and
any sound effects. Narration uses one Audiobooks project and one narrator,
Albary, with Eleven v4. The approved seven paragraphs are the `voiceover`
values of `timeline.json`, in scene order. Project/chapter identifiers are
recorded in `audioProduction`; all seven original paragraphs and the original
ElevenLabs Music file have now been imported. No separate SFX are used.

The earlier locally synthesized score is historical and is no longer loaded.
Do not run `make_score.py` for this revision or use it as a fallback. No audio
is generated by the render pipeline. Silent stills remain available while
original files are pending; video render fails closed unless originals validate.

Import the original Audiobooks Paragraphs ZIP and retain that ZIP outside Git.
Copy each selected paragraph byte-for-byte to these ignored paths:

- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/01-hook.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/02-boundary.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/03-brand.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/04-route.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/05-noir.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/06-mechanism.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/narration/07-closing.mp3`
- `public/audio/elevenlabs/music/background.mp3`
- Optional original SFX: `public/audio/elevenlabs/sfx/transition.mp3`,
  `click.mp3` or `reveal.mp3`.

Use the actual export extension (`mp3`, `wav`, or `m4a`). Preserve original
filenames separately as `sourceName`; no splitting a mixed export, speech
trimming, acceleration or time stretching. Measure originals with ffprobe and
hash their bytes with SHA-256. `FFPROBE_PATH` selects a full binary when it is
not on PATH. Original durations, not word counts, determine the final edit.

`timeline.audio` is the placement and provenance authority. Add one entry per
original paragraph, one per music file and one per SFX placement:

```json
{
  "id": "voice-hook",
  "kind": "narration",
  "file": "audio/elevenlabs/narration/01-hook.mp3",
  "provider": "ElevenLabs",
  "sourceName": "actual-original-export-name.mp3",
  "sha256": "actual-64-character-sha256",
  "sourceDurationSeconds": 3.2,
  "startFrame": 18,
  "durationInFrames": 96,
  "gain": 1,
  "sceneId": "hook",
  "voice": "Albary",
  "model": "Eleven v4",
  "leadInFrames": 18,
  "tailFrames": 18
}
```

The numbers above illustrate the schema; they are not measured evidence.
Music and SFX use the common fields without scene/voice/model/lead/tail fields.
Gain controls only the rendered mix, never modifies originals. Every track
uses `ceil(measured seconds * fps)` frames and must fit the composition.
Narration start is `scene.startFrame + leadInFrames`, and lead + original
length + tail must fit its scene. No track repeats or loops automatically.

The measured edit is 2,402 frames / 80.066667 seconds at 30 fps. Scene lengths
are 210, 270, 240, 450, 330, 480 and 422 frames. Each original narration uses
a 9-frame lead and at least a 9-frame tail inside its scene. The music is
80.039167 seconds, rounded up to 2,402 frames so its original encoder padding
is retained. Music plays once at gain 0.06 with a frame-derived 45-frame fade
in and 90-frame fade out. Narration gain is 1 with no fades, clipping or speed
change. These are starting mix settings, pending actual encoded-mix analysis
and listening review. No generated/synthetic SFX or old score cues are loaded.
The approved 110-second cap remains available; no further extension is needed.

Render preflight checks originals, hashes, measured lengths, requested narrator,
model, complete paragraph coverage and music. It does not prove ElevenLabs
account selections, pronunciation, mix quality or listening review: the operator
records those separately. Keep original ZIPs, audio and large renders ignored.
