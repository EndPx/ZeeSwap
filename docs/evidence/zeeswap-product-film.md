# ZeeSwap motion demo

The requested `create-demo-motion-hackathon` workflow produced an original
80-second, 1920×1080, 30-fps MP4 with English on-screen copy, an original
instrumental score and purposeful UI/cursor/data-path animation. No voice or
paid generation was used. The approved original Zee mark/mascot, web fonts,
palette, fixed testnet rate and privacy/custody boundaries remain intact.

Editable sources are in `tools/demo-video/`. Its `timeline.json` controls the
master, scene durations and audio cues. `DESIGN.md`, `STORYBOARD.md`, `AUDIO.md`
and `PROVENANCE.md` describe the creative contract and verification limits.
The dependency lock is pinned. Raw/generated audio, movie, GIF and QA images
remain outside public Git; the original score is reproducible from source.

Typechecking and four focused timing/motion/label tests passed. The skill's
timeline auditor passed without warnings. FFprobe measured 80.000 seconds,
and complete FFmpeg decoding passed with video and audio present. Two renderings
of frame 1080 matched exactly; repeated score generation matched exactly.
Eight source frames and sixteen sampled encoded frames were visually inspected.
Full human playback/listening was not performed; those checks are not implied.
